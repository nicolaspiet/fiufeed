# Code of Conduct

## Summary

Participation in this project should remain respectful, direct, and constructive.

## Expected Behavior

- discuss ideas without personal attacks
- focus criticism on code, behavior, and decisions
- assume good faith unless there is strong evidence otherwise
- be clear and specific when reporting bugs or reviewing changes

## Unacceptable Behavior

- harassment or intimidation
- discriminatory language
- doxxing or privacy violations
- bad-faith spam, trolling, or intentionally disruptive behavior

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or block participation when behavior is clearly harmful to the project or its contributors.
